# Failover Mechanism During Outages

Based on the provided context, the failover mechanism operates at multiple levels:

## Database Level
The primary database uses a **multi-AZ configuration with synchronous replication** to a standby replica in a separate availability zone. During an outage, **automatic failover promotes the replica to primary within 30 seconds**, achieving an RTO of under one minute. Point-in-time recovery is supported through continuous WAL archival.

## Load Balancer Level
Regional load balancers perform **health checks at 5-second intervals** to detect unhealthy backends. Unhealthy backends are **removed from rotation within 15 seconds**, allowing traffic to be redirected to healthy nodes. The system maintains **fully stateless routing** (except for stateful upload flows with sticky sessions), permitting traffic to be directed to any available node.

## Incident Coordination
Beyond technical failover, **incident commanders coordinate response via a runbook playbook with auto-paging to on-call engineers**, ensuring human oversight and response during failover events.

## Overall Recovery Targets
The disaster recovery procedures define an **RPO of 5 minutes and an RTO of 30 minutes**, with **cross-region replication maintaining warm standbys** and **quarterly game-day exercises validating the failover runbook**.
